This file is what makes Snag portable.

While it sits next to snag.exe, Snag keeps everything it owns in the "data"
folder beside the executable: your settings, and any yt-dlp it installs for
you. It writes nothing to AppData, the registry, or anywhere else on the
machine, so you can carry this folder on a USB stick or delete it and be done.

Delete this file and Snag reverts to the normal behaviour, storing settings in
%APPDATA%\Snag\config instead.

One thing worth knowing before you share this folder with anyone: the "data"
folder holds your settings, and those record real paths on your machine, such
as your download folder. Delete the "data" folder before passing the folder on
if you would rather not share that.

Snag still needs ffmpeg for merging and conversion. If you want the portable
copy to be fully self-contained, drop ffmpeg.exe into the "data" folder and
point at it in settings > advanced.
